# Getting started

[Português](../pt/getting-started.md)

## 1. Turn sand into electronics

1. Use a vanilla **Brush** on ordinary **Sand**, holding use for about two seconds. Each completed attempt has a **35%** chance to produce **Silica Rich Sand**. The sand block is consumed; failed attempts drop ordinary sand.
2. Smelt Silica Rich Sand into **Silicon Dust**.
3. Smelt Silicon Dust into a **Silicon Boule**.
4. Craft the boule with a supported sword to obtain **12 Silicon Wafers**. The sword loses one durability point rather than being consumed.
5. Surround a wafer with four gold ingots in a cross to make a **Doped Wafer**.
6. Combine wafers with Gold Wire, redstone and copper to make **Basic Circuits** and **Basic Capacitors**. See [key recipes](recipes.md).

Keep iron blocks, gold, copper, redstone and diamonds available: even the first machines require substantial vanilla resources.

## 2. Set up a small powered workshop

Craft a **Basic Coal Generator**, a **Basic Refiner**, a battery and suitable energy cables. Give the refiner a supported input recipe and supply FE. Add a **Basic Extractor** when your power supply can sustain it.

A Basic Refiner needs **500 FE/t**, while one Basic Coal Generator exports **250 FE/t**. A battery can bridge shortages temporarily, but cannot make one generator sustain that load forever. A Basic Extractor needs **2,000 FE/t** before modifiers.

Watch the machine's energy and stability, keep output slots clear, and use [modules and Flux Crystals](machines.md) for maintenance. The [Configurator](controller.md) tunes calibration; the Machine Controller becomes your automation panel later.

## 3. Gather dimensional materials

Vanilla Creepers can drop **Unstable Particles**. Endermen can drop **Void Dust**, **Void Fragments** and, more rarely, **Void Bars**. The base added-drop chances are 50%, 70%, 20% and 5% respectively; Looting improves them.

**Stellar Flower** can be processed in a refiner to obtain **Stellar Essence**.

Stable and Unstable Particle ores generate in the Overworld between **Y −64 and −30**.

## 4. Approach your first Rift

Overworld Rifts can appear before you have advanced machines. Their tier determines the danger; an early discovery of a high-tier Rift does not mean you are ready to fight it.

Make a **Stable Orb** and hold use near an ordinary Rift for five seconds to seal it. Its **Rift Residue** and possible **Rift Core** become useful in later recipes. Read [Rifts](rifts.md) and [Breaches](breaches.md) before treating an anomaly as a permanent farm.
