# Dimensional Breach collapse and Guardian

## Lifecycle

Per-stage growth rolls now use 18% for Minor → Reality, 15% for Reality → Major and 10% for Major → Breach. Each stage still rolls only once; most Rifts close normally. Existing rolled decisions are preserved.

Breach Stability starts at 100%. It loses 100 percentage points over 36,000 loaded ticks (30 minutes at 20 TPS). The scheduler runs once per second with a persisted fractional accumulator. Unloaded chunks do not accrue instability. Active sealing pauses deterioration. Destroying anchors exposes the existing Stable Orb sealing interaction; it does not restore stability.

| Stability | State | Terrain checks per scheduled tick | Local hostile limit | Wave size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 61–100% | Relatively Stable | 8 | 36 | 3–5 |
| 26–60% | Unstable Breach | 16 | 46 | 5–7 |
| 1–25% | Critical Instability | 24 | 56 | 7–9 |
| 0% | Dimensional Collapse | Collapse transition | No new waves after transition | Guardian |

Only one mob spawn is attempted per scheduled second. The global terrain budget remains 24 candidate checks per tick, with round-robin scheduling among loaded rifts. Existing BlockEntity, structure-material and player-placement protections remain. Maximum affected block count increases from 18,000 to 24,000 and 30,000.

A successful collapse spawn closes the breach, removes the central rupture and anchors, and preserves existing creatures and terrain. Collapse does not grant sealing rewards. If the center has no safe loaded space or the world is Peaceful, the zero-stability breach waits instead of deleting the event or spawning a boss unsafely.

## Guardian

One `chaosmod:dimensional_guardian` entity supports all nine affinities. No biome spawns, spawn eggs or `/tiermob` entries are added. A Guardian without a persisted collapse binding removes itself. Its health, tier, home, cooldowns, shield, accumulated energy and ability phase survive saving. The Rift stores its Guardian UUID and accepted-spawn flag so ordinary reloads do not duplicate it.

Native model: 2.4×3.8 block collision bounds; exposed rotating core, floating containment armor, segmented ring and orbiting shards. The same model is tinted for every tier. Shield phases expand the ring. Attack windups charge the core before the ability executes.

- Unstable: irregular cooldowns and small explosive pulses without terrain destruction.
- Stable: temporary 70% damage-reduction shield.
- Void: pulls nearby players and attempts a short safe teleport.
- Stellar: plasma projectiles and temporary overheated zones, limited to four zones.
- Duality: alternating defensive and offensive phases.
- Darklight: absorbs part of incoming damage and releases a stored-energy discharge.
- Nova: stores impact energy and emits radial knockback waves.
- Zenith: rotates nearby projectile trajectories and attempts a short safe player displacement.
- Chaos: cycles through the eight abilities and accumulates energy.

Teleports require loaded space, world-border checks, solid ground and collision/liquid clearance. Combat abilities do not destroy terrain or load remote chunks. Player targeting excludes creative/spectator players through normal goals and ability filters.

The Guardian uses the established equipment references (including Zenith level 21 and Chaos axe) with a higher health budget and stronger attacks. It has its own combat logic instead of stacking all generic tier-mob abilities. Chaos Sword instant-kill behavior remains intact.

## Core and data generation

Each tier has a separate `<tier>_dimensional_guardian_core` item. Exactly one matching core drops on death under the normal mob-loot game rule. There are no crafting recipes, Curios integration or accessory effects yet. Item models and the empty base entity loot table are generated by datagen; the affinity-dependent drop is emitted by the entity.

The transparent 64×64 grayscale item sprite was generated with the built-in image tool and resized locally with user permission. Runtime item coloring follows the existing tier bar textures (rather than the pale geological highlight palette). See `guardian-core-art.json` for the prompt and source asset path.

## Migration and validation

All new saved fields are optional. Older Breaches start at 100% stability with no accumulated unloaded time; existing rift IDs, sizes, states, anchors, terrain history and sealing progress are retained. Terrain history loading accepts the new expanded block limits. Client and server must both install the update because new entity and item registries were added.

GameTests cover old-data defaults, stage boundaries, increased activity, saved fractional drain, exactly one Guardian after collapse/reload, Guardian affinity persistence, model size and all nine core drops. The model also receives a structural bake audit. Visual appearance and combat feel still require an in-game playtest.

## Creative command

`/realityrift create breach nova 25` creates a Nova Breach at 25% stability. The original affinity-first order also works: `/realityrift create nova breach 25`. Omit the percentage to start at 100%. The integer must be between 0 and 100 and is only accepted for Breaches. Zero requests collapse on the next scheduled loaded tick, subject to safe placement and non-Peaceful difficulty.

## Chaos natural Rift unlock

Rifts are restricted to the Overworld, including their central entity and terrain/spawn processing. Entering a Chaos Reality permanently unlocks natural Chaos Rifts for that saved world (shared by multiplayer players). The check covers dimension entry, login inside a Chaos region and movement between regions. Legacy saves default to locked until a visit is detected. Chaos receives approximately 0.02% of affinity rolls after unlocking, rarer than Zenith. Stable remains excluded from natural Rifts; creative commands retain explicit tier selection. Existing creatures in Realities are unaffected.
