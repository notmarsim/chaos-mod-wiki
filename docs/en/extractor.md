# Reality Extractor and the route to Chaos

[Português](../pt/extractor.md)

The Reality Extractor fills a **Reality Core** with samples from different Realities of one tier. This is separate from the Rift Core obtained by closing anomalies.

## Prepare the equipment

1. Produce **Reality Condensate** in the [Zenith Accelerator](accelerator.md).
2. Craft a blank Reality Core using **13 condensates** in the Void Station's recipe.
3. Craft the Reality Extractor. Its [9×9 recipe](recipes.md) consumes a Reality Core and produces **one extractor**.
4. Craft additional blank cores for collection and prepare a portable FE supply.

## Complete a core

Place the extractor **inside a Reality**, insert a compatible incomplete core and supply energy. An extraction requires **1,200 paid work ticks at a base 2,000,000 FE/t**: a nominal 2.4 billion FE before calibration effects.

Each completed extraction supplies one of **three required samples**. All three must come from **distinct Realities of the same tier**. A world-saved record prevents extracting another sample from the same Reality, including with a different machine. An incomplete core of another tier is refused; a finished core needs no more samples.

Extraction costs **50 percentage points of Reality stability**. Check stability before starting and avoid mining away the budget you need to leave safely. Breaking the extractor drops its inventory and resets unfinished processing; already collected core samples remain on their item.

## Craft the Chaotic Catalyst

Complete one core for each of these eight tiers:

**Unstable · Stable · Void · Stellar · Duality · Darklight · Nova · Zenith**

That requires **24 distinct successful collections**. The base extraction energy alone totals **57.6 billion FE**, excluding the accelerator, portal, transport and modifiers.

Use the eight completed cores and Zenith in the Chaotic Catalyst recipe. A blank core or a core with only one or two samples does not satisfy it. A Chaos core is not required to unlock Chaos.

Sneak-right-click the [Gateway Terminal](gateway.md) with the catalyst to enter Chaos mode. There, Chaos creature fragments and exclusive Reality materials contribute to the final Chaotic crafting progression.
