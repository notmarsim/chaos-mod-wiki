# Dimensional Breaches

[Português](../pt/breaches.md)

A Dimensional Breach is the maximum Rift stage: a much larger rupture, more rapid terrain contamination and repeated small waves of creatures. Unlike an ordinary Rift, it remains an event to resolve instead of simply weakening and closing on its normal timer.

## Event sequence

1. **Incursion:** the first waves bring creatures of the Breach's tier.
2. **Expansion:** the affected area grows while the event remains active.
3. **Anchors:** after about **ten seconds of active processing**, **3–5 living Reality Anchors** appear around the center, roughly **24–46 blocks away** horizontally.
4. **Exposed:** destroy all Anchors to make the center sealable.
5. **Sealing:** hold use with a Stable Orb near the center while final waves continue.

## Anchor effects

| Tier | Main effect |
|---|---|
| Void | Pulls nearby entities |
| Stellar | Fires projectiles |
| Darklight | Applies darkness and strengthens nearby monsters |
| Nova | Emits area damage pulses |
| Zenith | Produces levitation / local gravity disruption |

Other tiers use the shared movement-disturbance behavior. These are living combat targets, distinct from the craftable **Dimensional Anchor** chunk loader. They are intended to be defeated by player attacks; environmental damage is not a substitute for the encounter.

## Seal the exposed center

Once the display shows **zero remaining Anchors**, hold use with a **Stable Orb** within **six blocks** of the center. Sealing requires **15 seconds** of progress. Leaving range or releasing use **pauses** progress instead of resetting it. Several players do not multiply sealing speed.

The final waves still require attention. One orb is consumed when sealing completes in Survival, the rupture implodes, and its additional spawning ends. Existing mobs and altered terrain remain.

## Rewards

A sealed Breach guarantees **one Rift Core**, **32 to `64 + 4 × tier index` Rift Residue**, and **`500 + 100 × tier index` XP points**. Tier indices follow the [Rift reward table](rifts.md).

Examples: Unstable gives 32–64 residue and 500 XP; Zenith gives 32–92 residue and 1,200 XP. Rewards are dropped at the former center. Make room in your inventory and collect them before leaving.

Leaving an ordinary Rift open preserves its farming opportunities, but accepts the small escalation risk. Ordinary tiered creatures retain their normal loot; the Breach reward is a separate reason to finish the event.
