# Welcome to Chaos Mod

[Português](../pt/index.md)

Chaos Mod combines demanding machine progression with dangerous dimensional exploration. Build a power supply, refine materials, automate maintenance, and develop the infrastructure that lets you travel into Realities.

Rifts introduce these dimensions much earlier: temporary anomalies bring tiered terrain and creatures into the Overworld. You can seal them for materials, or leave them active for combat and farming—with a small risk of escalation into a Dimensional Breach.
