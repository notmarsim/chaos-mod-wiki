# Industrial Refinery

## Construction and capacity

The former eight-chamber Stellar Core ceiling has been removed. The Core determines the maximum recipe/chamber tier only. Operational capacity is the minimum of installed chambers, 4 per Material Distribution Unit, 4 per Energy Distribution Unit and 6 per Stability Regulation Unit. Thus 111 chambers and 31 of each unit provide 111 operational chambers. Actual processing separately requires ingredients, FE distribution, stored energy and output space.

Rectangular structures retain a minimum dimension of five, without a fixed maximum. Edges must be casing and the shell must be closed. Every internal component must directly touch a connected conduit. Components do not forward connectivity to other components.

Validation proceeds across ticks through shell discovery, surface checks, chunk-section interior scanning, conduit BFS and connectivity checks. Empty sections are skipped; only components are cached. Bounded audits and block-change events invalidate the cache. Unloaded required chunks suspend operation without force loading. Server configuration exposes validation, audit, chamber-visit and cached-component budgets; there is no size-based efficiency penalty.

## Inventory and terminal

Three fixed module slots are labelled Upgrades. A separate paged stabilizer inventory provides two active slots per Stability Regulation Unit. Additional units increase slot count, never crystal potency. Removing units retains stacks for withdrawal. The former shared flux slot migrates into stabilizer storage without loss.

The terminal separates installed, operational and currently processing Chambers, and reports validation phase, inspected positions and connectivity errors. Energy accounting uses long values with saturating aggregate-capacity arithmetic.

## Ports and upgrades

Use the Configurator on the unified Industrial Port to cycle item input, item output, energy input and stabilizer input. The central indicator changes color. Legacy port IDs remain registered for existing saves and convert into the corresponding configurable port mode.

Core and Chamber upgrades progress from Stellar through Darklight, Nova, Zenith and Chaos. Apply the matching next-tier upgrade in place. Dropped blocks preserve their tier, and creative variants are available. Core upgrade recipes use the current-tier refiner and circuit; Chambers use the current-tier refiner/wires and previous-tier circuit.

All recipes are generated by ModRecipeProvider.industrialRecipes:

- Casing: Void Orb surrounded by 8 Machine Casings, yields 8.
- Port: Void Circuit surrounded by 8 Industrial Casings.
- Conduit: Void Cable surrounded by 8 Industrial Casings, yields 8.
- Material Unit: Chest surrounded by 8 Industrial Casings.
- Energy Unit: Void Battery surrounded by 8 Industrial Casings.
- Stability Unit: Empowered Flux Crystal surrounded by 8 Industrial Casings.
- Glass: any Forge glass with 4 Industrial Casings in a cross, yields 4.
- Core: a 5x5 casing border around 8 Stellar Refiners and a central Stellar Circuit.
- Chamber: a 5x5 casing border around a central Stellar Refiner, corner Stellar Wires and cardinal Void Circuits.

The Terminal uses a 5x5 casing border with four Void Orbs in the inner corners and five Void Circuits in a cross.

## Models and verification

Editable Blockbench sources and previews are in art/models/industrial_refinery. tools/blockbench/build_industrial_refinery.py authors and exports through Blockbench MCP; industrial_designs.py defines geometry. Parts use ingredient-based palettes and tier variants. Casing panels match on all six sides, conduits have dynamic arms and ports have mode-specific indicators. Casing/Terminal accents are black, Energy Unit internals are yellow, and Darklight variants follow the magenta tier palette. Layered geometry uses recessed joints and cutout rendering to prevent coplanar flicker; glass has a thin clear frame.

GameTests include a 101x101x101 shell, 111 operational Chambers, disconnected components, escrow persistence and port capability changes. In-game visual verification remains deferred at the user's request.


## Component connectors and upgrade sprites

Industrial conduits use black details. Cores, Chambers and distribution/regulation units have six visual connector states that extend toward adjacent conduits only. Their connector models use world directions independently of machine facing. Removing a conduit retracts the corresponding connector. Bounded structure validation refreshes older component states without changing tier, inventories or connectivity rules.

Industrial Glass uses vanilla glass sound properties. Core/Chamber Upgrade items use generated 2D PNG sprites for Darklight, Nova, Zenith and Chaos. Original generated images and complete prompts are archived in `art/items/industrial_upgrades/simple/generation.json`. Runtime textures are user-approved 64x64 RGBA downscales under `textures/item`; datagen links them using item/generated rather than a Blockbench block model. `tools/import_industrial_upgrade_art.py` reproduces the resize from the archived originals.


Industrial Glass now uses vanilla glass skylight propagation, zero light blocking and neutral ambient shading. Its frame model disables ambient occlusion and directional face shading. Connected or stacked glass panels retain ordinary daylight transmission without emitting light, while collision and existing registry/state IDs are preserved.
Existing glass in validated shells also refreshes its cached skylight sources and schedules a lighting check during bounded validation, so stored lighting can update without replacing blocks or loading chunks.
